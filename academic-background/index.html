Research Interests

My research mainly revolves around nanomaterial synthesis and nanoelectronics fabrication, including low-dimensional materials for transistors and memory devices, GaN-based LED arrays for optogenetics research with neural cells, and solution-processed electronics for fundamental logic computations. These experiences have solidified my expertise in the fundamentals of semiconductor device physics across a wide range of components.

Skills

Computer competencies

OriginLab, DigitalMicrograph, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, MATLAB, Python, LabVIEW, Blender, SolidWorks, KLayout, QuantumATK, etc.

Fabrication

Familiar with all the common fabrication processes for nanoelectronics, including (but not limited to) photolithography (both traditional mask manual aligner and Heidelberg maskless aligner), electron-beam lithography (heavy user of both Raith and Elionix systems), wet etching with HF/BOE/KOH etc. and dry etching such as reactive ion etching (RIE) and plasma-enhanced ICP-RIE, wet/dry oxidation, metal deposition (e-beam evaporator, AJA sputtering system, thermal evaporator, etc.), atomic layer deposition (ALD), and plasma-enhanced chemical vapor deposition (PECVD).

Materials characterization and measurements

Nanophoton Raman, atomic force microscopy (Cypher AFM), scanning electron microscopy (Hitachi SEM), focused ion beam (Thermo Scios 2 DualBeam SEM/FIB), thermogravimetric analysis (TGA), X-ray diffraction (XRD), X-ray photoelectron spectroscopy (XPS), Fourier-transform infrared spectroscopy (FTIR), CHI650D electrochemical workstation, transmission electron microscopy (TEM), scanning transmission electron microscopy (STEM), Micromanipulator probe station (Keysight EasyEXPERT), CRX-6.5K probe station, Brunauer–Emmett–Teller (BET) analysis, gas chromatography, etc.

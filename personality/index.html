Personality

Relaxed, optimistic, hands-on — and always up for a new challenge.

I’ve got a relaxed and easy-going personality, always up for lending a helping hand to folks. My optimism keeps me buzzing, and I’m all in when it comes to embracing change and taking on challenges. I’m a hands-on enthusiast, absolutely loving experimenting and getting my hands dirty with all sorts of equipment. Exploring new horizons is a true passion of mine, and I happen to excel at it. How, you wonder? Well, my knack for staying meticulously organized and my speedy learning abilities make it all possible!

When I’m not diving into my academic adventures, I’m soaking up life’s pleasures. I’m a big animal lover, and I’m lucky to have two adorable cats keeping me company.

The great outdoors is where I recharge — it brings that fresh vibe and keeps my mental game strong! I’m also quite the culinary enthusiast. For me, cooking is akin to conducting experiments: a realm where creativity reigns, guided by a set of well-honed principles to ensure a delicious outcome.

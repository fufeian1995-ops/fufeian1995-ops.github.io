Senior Product Engineer · Analog Devices, Inc.

Fufei An

What I know, I know deeply. What I don't, I learn quickly.

I provide engineering support to new and mature product lines in volume production — monitoring and identifying yield-enhancement opportunities, performing root-cause analysis, and working with design, process, test, reliability, and manufacturing engineering to put solutions in place. I develop and sustain process improvements that reduce production costs and increase yields.

I earned my Ph.D. in Materials Science and Engineering at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign, advised by Prof. Qing Cao. My doctoral research focused on nanomaterial synthesis and nanoelectronics fabrication, including atomically thin amorphous carbon films synthesized from solution precursors for novel low-dimensional transistors and memristors. Other projects included neuromorphic computing and solution-processed, printable electronics.
